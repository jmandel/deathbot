BiPAP is the step below a breathing tube. So the useful question is: did anyone talk to you about what happens if the mask isn't enough? That's the decision people usually haven't been given a chance to think about.

It doesn't fix the lungs — it holds things steady while the actual problem, the flare, is treated. Some people need it a day or two and come off. Some need it much longer, and for long stretches they put a shorter tube in through the neck.

**Deathbot:**

One honest gap first: I don't have a figure for how many of those 31 got home and stayed there. What I have is broader.
