# Brief: blind read of sixteen simulated conversations

`research/tone-review/blind3/M01.md` to `M12.md` are whole conversations between Deathbot and a simulated person (Frank, 74, COPD; Rosa, 81, heart failure, wants full treatment). They come from three different underlying models running the same prompt; you are not told which. The person is played by another model; judge only Deathbot.

Judge Deathbot against best practice (Serious Illness Conversation Guide, REMAP, `research/episode-180-cacp-transcript.md`) and the problems found last round (`research/tone-review/blind-astra.md`, `blind-argon.md`): responding to what the person said; pacing and length on a phone; asking before numbers and keeping figures' meaning straight; equal scrutiny (no approving or disapproving a preference; checking understanding either way; with Rosa, helping make full treatment effective rather than steering to limits); unsupported reassurance; the safety exchange and what follows it; whether the draft turn stays a draft review; buttons (helpful at real choices, absent as menus); stock phrases and rituals; invented content.

Deliver, in under about 1,100 words:
1. A score from 1 to 10 for each conversation, with one sentence and one short excerpt.
2. The groups you can see (there are three, four conversations each), which conversations fall in each, and what distinguishes them; then rank the groups.
3. The three most important remaining problems, with excerpts.

Review only: edit no files except your own report.
