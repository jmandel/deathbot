## DEATHBOT
A DNR stops **only** the emergency attempt to bring you back after your heart or breathing has completely stopped. That means no chest compressions to crack ribs, no electric shocks, and no emergency resuscitation drugs.

A DNR does **not** mean "do not treat." It changes nothing about your everyday care while you are alive:

- You still go to the hospital for pneumonia or bad COPD flare-ups.
- You still get IV antibiotics, steroids, oxygen, nebulizers, and BiPAP.
- You still get pain relief, comfort medicines, and full medical care.

It only takes effect at the exact moment of death.
