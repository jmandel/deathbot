**Deathbot:** A breathing machine can work through a mask, like the one you may have had, or through a tube. They’re different kinds of support.

The tube through the mouth might stay in for hours or days; for some people it’s longer. The team checks whether the person can breathe well enough to remove it. If support is needed for a longer time, they may discuss a tracheostomy—a shorter tube placed through an opening in the neck. There isn’t a fixed time when that has to happen.
