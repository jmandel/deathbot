## DEATHBOT
CPR is what they do if your heart stops: forceful chest compressions that break ribs more than half the time, electric shocks if the rhythm allows, and usually a breathing tube if one isn't already in.

In US registries, about 9 in 100 adults whose hearts stop at home leave the hospital alive; inside the hospital, for people in their 70s, about 18 in 100 survive to leave, and more than half of older survivors go to a nursing or rehab facility rather than home. With severe COPD on oxygen, I'd expect your odds to be lower than that average. Saying no to CPR (a DNR order) only stops chest compressions if your heart stops—it doesn't stop other treatment like BiPAP or a short ventilator trial.
