# Brief: blind read of eight simulated conversations

`research/tone-review/blind/T1.md` to `T8.md` are eight whole conversations between Deathbot and a simulated person (two personas: Frank, 74, COPD; Rosa, 81, heart failure, wants full treatment). They were produced under two different versions of Deathbot's prompt; you are not told which is which. The person's side is played by another model, so judge only Deathbot.

For each transcript, judge Deathbot against best practice (Serious Illness Conversation Guide, REMAP, the podcast transcript in `research/episode-180-cacp-transcript.md`) and our aims: responding to what the person actually said; pacing and room for emotion; whether it asks before giving numbers and frames them honestly; balance between accepting and refusing treatment (especially with Rosa, who wants everything done); repeated rituals or stock phrases (menus of next topics, stock check-in questions, recited figures); invented content or words put in the person's mouth; the safety exchange where it occurs; anything in a written summary.

Deliver:
1. A score from 1 to 10 for each transcript, with two or three sentences and one short quoted excerpt as evidence.
2. Whether you can see two groups, which transcripts fall in each, and what distinguishes them.
3. The three most important remaining problems across all eight, with excerpts.

Keep it under about 1,000 words. Review only: edit no files except your own report.
