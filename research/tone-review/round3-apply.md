# Applied Round 3 blind-review fixes (`round3-apply`)

1. **`skill/deathbot/guide/conversation.md` ("Offering ready-made answers") — Restored buttons for concrete choices**
   - Stated positively when buttons help: include tappable replies whenever the message asks the person to choose between concrete options (whether they want numbers, which picture to see, whether to keep going or stop, or whether a reflection or draft is right).
   - Kept the protections concisely: do not invent a menu of next topics after an explanation; leave open-ended questions without buttons unless the person is stuck and needs balanced examples; no treatment decisions right after an explanation, numbers or a picture; always include a way to qualify or correct when checking a reflection or draft; never offer buttons on a safety turn.

2. **`skill/deathbot/guide/conversation.md` and `skill/deathbot/guide/voice.md` — Equal scrutiny and no evaluation of choices**
   - Updated `Learning first, deciding later` in `conversation.md` (and `No stock sympathy and no slogans` in `voice.md`) so Deathbot does not evaluate or praise a preference in either direction, reflects it in the person's words, and checks the understanding it rests on before treating it as settled (including whether they have ruled out a temporary step such as a short rehabilitation stay that could still reach their goal).
   - Made explicit that with someone who wants full treatment, Deathbot helps make that wish effective and prepares their proxy to carry it out, and that exploring limits is only one possible thread, not the destination of the conversation.

3. **`skill/deathbot/guide/conversation.md` — Honest uncertainty instead of unsupported reassurance**
   - Updated proxy preparation in `Learning first, deciding later` and `Who speaks for you` to check any backup's willingness and ability to follow wishes they personally disagree with, and stated that comfort comes from honest description and practical preparation, never from promising how a hospital stay or withdrawal will unfold or how family members will behave.

4. **`skill/deathbot/guide/safety.md` ("A passive wish to be dead") — Keeping treatable suffering in view after a "no"**
   - Clarified the post-denial path so that when the person says no—including when they preemptively rule out self-harm while answering what makes life hard and try to change the subject—Deathbot does not jump straight back to planning: it first responds to the specific hardship they named (breathlessness, pain, exhaustion, grief, isolation) and, when a clinician or support service can help, states that it is worth raising with their doctor before returning to the planning thread.

5. **`skill/deathbot/SKILL.md`, `skill/deathbot/templates/summary.md`, and `skill/deathbot/guide/conversation.md` — Separating draft review from the legal-form briefing**
   - Updated **The path** in `SKILL.md` (while preserving all approved legal claims verbatim), `templates/summary.md`, and `Capture` in `conversation.md` so the turn that presents a draft of "What matters to me" stays focused on reviewing the draft (plus at most the single wrap-up sentence if they are finishing), saving form links, witness rules, and distribution steps until they have reviewed the draft or asked for them.

6. **`skill/deathbot/guide/information.md` ("Numbers") — Preventing shifting denominators and checking repeated figures**
   - Extended the final bullet under `Numbers` so each figure's group, outcome, and period stay fixed, a figure is never restated as measuring a different group or outcome (such as confusing survival to discharge with restarting the heart, or ICU-ventilated patients with everyone who has the illness), and when the person repeats a figure back or bases a choice on it, Deathbot checks which group and outcome they think it describes.
