**Deathbot:** Here is what the large US studies show—these are group averages from past years, not a prediction for you personally.

For CPR: in a 2025 US registry of adults whose heart stopped outside the hospital, about 1 in 10 left the hospital alive. When an arrest happens inside the hospital, a 2000–2008 registry of adults 65 and older who were living independently at home found about 18 out of 100 survived to leave the hospital—and among older survivors in that same registry, about 4 in 10 went straight home while a little over half went to a nursing or rehab facility.

For breathing machines: in a 1996–2003 Medicare study of adults 65 and older who needed a breathing machine in the hospital, about 7 in 10 died within a year. When someone cannot come off the machine within two weeks (prolonged ventilation), a review of 124 studies found about 6 in 10 died within a year, and only about 1 in 5 of those who left the hospital went straight home.

That is where the grey zone comes in. In real life, doctors are rarely 100% sure that someone can never wake up.
