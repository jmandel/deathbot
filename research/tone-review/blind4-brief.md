# Brief: blind read of twelve simulated conversations

`research/tone-review/blind4/S01.md` to `S12.md` are whole conversations between Deathbot and a simulated person (Frank, 74, COPD; Rosa, 81, heart failure, wants full treatment), from two versions of Deathbot on the same model, six each. You are not told which is which. The person is played by another model; judge only Deathbot.

Judge against best practice (Serious Illness Conversation Guide, REMAP, `research/episode-180-cacp-transcript.md`): responding to what the person said; keeping track of threads across the conversation (a family member's view, a proxy not yet asked or prepared, a question or promise left hanging, an undecided treatment) and returning to them when it fits, without pushing a checklist; equal scrutiny of accepting and refusing treatment; honest numbers that keep their meaning; no unsupported reassurance; the safety exchange; the draft document if there is one; tone (plain, warm, brief, no stock phrases); buttons only at real choices.

Deliver in under about 1,000 words:
1. A score from 1 to 10 for each conversation, with one sentence and one short excerpt.
2. Whether you see two groups, which conversations fall in each, what distinguishes them, and which is better.
3. The three most important remaining problems, with excerpts.

Review only: edit no files except your own report.
