**Yes—as a development plan, with unresolved launch requirements.** The revision now centers the actual product: helping someone understand trade-offs and communicate qualified preferences. The conversation loop, baseline comparison, stronger summary checks, narrower forms support, and observed-user launch gate materially improve it. Keeping personalized information, research, forms, and images is defensible within the owner’s constraints. The remaining weaknesses concern what counts as adequate evidence, what happens when something fails, and whether the launch gate actually prevents premature exposure.

1. **Personalization still lacks an operational boundary.** Labeling a claim “application to you” does not make an unsupported inference acceptable, yet unsupported prognosis blocks release. Define the distinction: permit conditional reasoning with explicit assumptions; prohibit individual probabilities or categorical predictions unsupported by an applicable validated method. When information is insufficient, describe how different plausible facts would change the answer. This preserves substantive personalization without mandatory clinician handoff.

2. **The launch gates specify activities, not acceptance criteria.** A clinician “reads” material; users attend sessions; neither requires findings to be resolved. Assign responsibility, document blocking findings, require fixes and retesting, and specify comprehension and pressure checks. Include qualified review of the two states’ form rules and mappings. Apply these gates before public patient-facing distribution, not merely before the owner’s social-media post; a public v0.1 can circulate independently.

3. **The safety table misses immediate-danger distinctions.** An attempt underway or suspected overdose needs emergency medical escalation, not just crisis counseling. “Stay with them” must not imply monitoring or guaranteed availability. Add separate imminent-danger and non-imminent-distress branches, including a nearby-person option and explicit limits on the bot’s capabilities. [NIMH guidance](https://www.nimh.nih.gov/health/publications/suicide-faq)

4. **The image evaluation cannot support its implied promotion path.** Five to ten general conversation sessions cannot establish that personalized images avoid undue persuasion. Keep the module and experimental label, but specify separate image-focused evaluation: comprehension, perceived predictive accuracy, distress, and preference changes with reasons. Do not treat this small pilot as clearance of persuasion risk. Replace mandatory “odds alongside” with applicable population estimates where available, otherwise an explicit statement that reliable odds are unavailable.

5. **Resumption can silently corrupt preferences.** Exportability does not establish that the model retains accurate working notes. Without adding save files, require a brief recap and reconfirmation after a substantial interruption, renewed clinical information, or uncertain context. Missing history must trigger reconstruction, never confident continuation.

The revision made two things worse: “each option gets its…odds” creates pressure to manufacture numbers, and the fixed instruction to “present the real set” can become another misleading menu—those options are neither universally available nor mutually exclusive. Make both conditional on the actual scenario. Section 12 also incorrectly treats exportable notes as resolving continuity; exporting and reliably resuming are different capabilities.
