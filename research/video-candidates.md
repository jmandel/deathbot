# YouTube video candidates for the fact cards

Date: 2026-11-14.

**How the search was done.** I searched YouTube's own results pages with about 90 queries. These covered each card topic and the named sources: PREPARE, ACP Decisions, The Conversation Project/IHI, NIA, National POLST, NHPCO, HFA, CAPC, health systems, medical societies, Mannix, BJ Miller, Frontline and Gawande. A few web searches were added. For each candidate I read the title, channel, length, upload date and the first part of the description from the watch page.

**How each video was checked.** Every video listed below returned HTTP 200 with a title from `https://www.youtube.com/oembed?url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=<ID>&format=json` on 2026-11-14. The watch page also reported `playableInEmbed: true`. Titles and channels are copied from the oEmbed response; some are shortened. Lengths come from the watch page.

**What was not checked.**
- I did not watch the videos all the way through. "What it shows" is based on the title, the description and who made it.
- Facts stated inside a video were not checked against the cards. Older videos (2010 to 2016) may give out-of-date numbers or rules. The Medicare hospice and POLST form details are the most likely to be out of date.

Columns: Title (linked) | Channel | Length | What it shows | Who made it | Fit and caveats.

## Channels worth following

- **National Institute on Aging** (@NIA): short, current, official animations. There are only a few end-of-life videos: advance directives, and palliative care versus hospice.
- **National POLST** and **Oregon POLST**: official POLST explainers. Most other uploads are clinician webinars.
- **The Conversation Project**: the proxy guide videos and conversation-starter clips. Non-commercial (IHI).
- **Get Palliative Care** (CAPC): short palliative-care definitions and patient stories, about 2014 to 2019.
- **Canadian Virtual Hospice**: many short interviews with clinicians and patients, including Joanne Lynn on trajectories. Canadian.
- **FRONTLINE PBS**: the full *Being Mortal* and short clips from it, with real patients.
- **National Cancer Institute**: the prognosis series, for cancer only.
- **My ICU Guide** (CARENET, Canada): short ICU and CPR decision-aid videos for families.
- **UCSF Division of Geriatrics, "GERI Videos"**: one-minute clips (POLST, advance care planning, end-of-life caregiving). They are new, and partly aimed at clinicians.
- **Hospice Nurse Julie** (Julie McFadden, RN): the largest and plainest body of "what dying looks like" videos, including the only real footage found. Caveats: a personal channel, business sponsorships and channel memberships, and some videos give direct advice. For example, "Dehydration at the End of Life" tells viewers to refuse IV fluids, so it was left out. Pick individual videos; do not endorse the whole channel.
- **Chelsey the Palliative PA**: very short, plain CPR, code-status and comfort-care clips (2025 to 2026). Personal channel.
- **The Intense MD**: a US ICU physician explaining code blue, life-support withdrawal and advance directives. Personal channel, and somewhat technical.

## What was not found

- **Real patient-facing footage of CPR on a real patient.** Only simulations were found: Oxford Medical Education's 360-degree video and Jeff Yoo's emergency-department simulation. News pieces show bystander CPR, which is a different setting.
- **ACP Decisions (Volandes) videos on YouTube.** The ACP Decisions website has a "Free Patient Content" page, but it plays videos on its own site and in the ACP Tools app, not on YouTube. The full library is behind a "Licensed Members" sign-in. The only YouTube copy found is a 2:23 news clip ([KPCC, "ACP Decisions video: Goals of care for advanced disease"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=g64zIKtB_0s), verified). It is a 2014 radio-station upload and was not listed above, because its rights status is unclear.
- **PREPARE for Your Care (UCSF) videos on YouTube.** The PREPARE program's videos are on prepareforyourcare.org, which can only show as a plain link. No official PREPARE YouTube channel or uploads were found. Only lectures by Dr. Sudore exist on YouTube, and all are 37 minutes or longer.
- **A short patient-facing video on time-limited trials.** Only a podcast with a legal angle (SF GWEP) and clinician podcasts (GeriPal, 47 min) were found.
- **A US patient-facing video on tracheostomy in older adults, or on long-term acute care hospitals (LTACHs).** Results were pediatric (Mass General Brigham, Cincinnati Children's), nursing-school material, a provider's ad (Kindred), or a commercial ICU consultant (Patrik Hutzel, excluded).
- **American Thoracic Society or AAHPM patient videos.** The ATS channel is clinician teaching only. No AAHPM patient videos were found.
- **Cleveland Clinic and Kaiser Permanente advance-care-planning videos on YouTube.** None were found. A Kaiser doctors' library talk exists (56 min).
- **Coalition to Transform Advanced Care (C-TAC) and CaringInfo patient videos.** None were found on YouTube.
- **Hospice Foundation of America videos made recently.** Its channel (hfahospice) has nothing newer than about 2014.
- **A US-made, non-personal-channel video of normal dying with real footage.** The best institutional explainers are UK (Mannix) and Canadian (Heart House Hospice), plus VNS Health in the US, which is narration only.

**Left out on purpose:**
- Law-firm and estate-planning videos.
- Fidelity and other financial firms.
- Lung Health: funnels viewers to a book.
- Patrik Hutzel: paid ICU consultancy.
- Texas Right to Life, End of Life Choices New York and Compassion & Choices: advocacy groups on one side of end-of-life policy.
- Ematai: frames DNR through religious law.
- Death Care Coach: a personal coaching business.
- First-aid and CPR-training videos: these teach how to do CPR, not what it is like.
- TV-drama clips.
