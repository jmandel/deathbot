# Template: Letter to the person who would speak for me

A letter from the person to their proxy. First person, warm, direct, in their voice. One page if possible. It should tell the proxy three things: what I am asking of you, what matters to me, and that I trust your judgement where this letter runs out. Draft it from the summary, show it to the person, and revise until it sounds like them.

---

Dear [name],

I'm asking you to be the person who speaks for me if I'm ever too sick to speak for myself. [If they have already asked: "Thank you for saying yes."] That means the doctors would turn to you, and I want you to be able to answer as me, not as yourself.

**What I care about most.** [Two or three sentences, their words.]

**What I'd want.** [The specific wishes, with their conditions intact.]

**What I'm not sure about.** [Open questions. "If it comes to X, I haven't made up my mind. Here's what I'd want you to think about."]

**If it's not clear what to do.** [How they want the proxy to decide: what to ask the doctors, what to weigh. For example: "Ask them what the best case, the worst case and the most likely case are. If the most likely case is that I wouldn't know you any more, let me go."]

**What I don't want you to carry.** [If they said it: that the proxy should not feel guilty, that the decision is theirs and the proxy is only delivering it.]

I may change my mind as things change, and I'll tell you if I do. I'd like to talk this through with you in person: [when/how, if they said].

[Sign-off in their style]
