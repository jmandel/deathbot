1. **Correct coverage exclusions.** Add the unrelated-care exception and inpatient/respite room-and-board exceptions. Clarify Medicare Advantage arrangements rather than implying everyone returns to Original Medicare. These errors can affect care-seeking and spending.

2. **Remove blanket survival reassurance.** “Comfort-focused care has not been shown to shorten life” combines different interventions and cannot establish the consequences of stopping an effective life-prolonging treatment. Replace with intervention-specific findings. Change “survived equally long” to “observational studies did not detect shorter survival”; do not use sedation studies to establish morphine safety.

3. **Correct the opening definition.** Comfort-focused care need not stop every treatment that slows disease: tumor-directed treatment can relieve symptoms. Define care by its goal, then separately explain hospice coverage and provider arrangements. [NCI](https://www.cancer.gov/about-cancer/advanced-cancer/care-choices/palliative-care-fact-sheet)

4. **Balance the alternatives.** No explicit recommendation appears, but “nothing is given up” makes concurrent treatment sound costless. Add treatment burdens, a time-limited trial with reassessment criteria, less intensive disease treatment, and comfort care without hospice enrollment. Explain potential benefits of hospital treatment alongside its burdens; retrospective bereavement associations cannot establish that choosing hospital care causes psychiatric illness.
