# Arizona (AZ)

Checked 2026-11-14.

## Naming someone to speak for you
- **Form:** "Health Care Power of Attorney" (the Attorney General's packet calls it "Durable Health Care Power of Attorney"), A.R.S. § 36-3221, with a sample form in § 36-3224. The sample form is optional: any writing that meets § 36-3221 is valid. The Arizona Attorney General's free Life Care Planning packet is the state's own copy. Single form: https://www.azag.gov/sites/default/files/2026-08/LCP%20-%20Healthcare%20POA_8.4.26.pdf. Full packet, Spanish versions and mailed packets: https://www.azag.gov/issues/elder-affairs/life-care-planning. The agent may also be given authority over funeral and disposition arrangements.
- **Signing:** dated and signed or marked by the person, then either notarized or witnessed by at least one adult. The witness or notary attests that the person appeared of sound mind and free from duress. If the person physically cannot sign, the notary or each witness must note that the person indicated the document reflects their wishes.
  - The named agent, and anyone directly involved in providing the person's health care at the time, may not be the witness or the notary.
  - If only one witness is used, that witness may not be related by blood, marriage or adoption, or entitled to any part of the estate by will or by law (§ 36-3221(C)-(D)).
- **Who cannot be the agent:** a person whose fiduciary license was suspended or revoked under § 14-5651, unless related to the person by blood, adoption or marriage (§ 36-3221(E)).
- **The agent's acceptance:** not required by statute.
- **Default decision-maker:** A.R.S. § 36-3231. In order: the spouse (unless legally separated); an adult child, with a majority of those reasonably available if there are several; a parent; the domestic partner, if the patient is unmarried; a brother or sister; then a close friend. A close friend is an adult who has shown special care and concern and knows the patient's health care views.

## Written instructions (living will)
- "Living Will", A.R.S. § 36-3261. The sample form is § 36-3262 (in the AG packet as "Living Will (End of Life Care)"). It can stand alone or be attached to the health care power of attorney. It uses the same signing rules as above (one qualified witness or a notary).
- In the sample form the person initials one or more statements: no life-prolonging treatment if terminal; no CPR, no "artificially administered food and fluids" and no hospital transfer if in a terminal condition, irreversible coma or persistent vegetative state; full treatment; or maximum life prolongation.
- **Pregnancy:** the sample form has a paragraph the person can initial. It says life-sustaining treatment is not withheld or withdrawn during a known pregnancy if the fetus could develop to live birth.

## Medical orders for the seriously ill (POLST-type)
- **Program:** Arizona POLST, run by Contexture, the state health information exchange, since 2012. Arizona uses the National POLST form. The AG packet links an Arizona copy at https://www.azag.gov/sites/default/files/2026-03/National_POLST_Form-AZ_Eng.pdf, and AzHDR posts it at https://azhdr.org/arizona-polst/.
- **Who signs:** a physician, physician assistant or nurse practitioner signs. The patient (or representative) also signs.
- Not confirmed: an Arizona statute or ADHS rule that gives POLST legal force for EMS. Arizona EMS law (§ 36-3251) is built on the orange Prehospital Medical Care Directive. A person who wants EMS to withhold CPR at home should also have the orange form.

## Out-of-hospital do-not-resuscitate order
- **Form:** "Prehospital Medical Care Directive" (A.R.S. § 36-3251). It must be printed on an orange background, letter or wallet size, and an orange-paper photocopy works. The AG offers a color version and a white version to print on orange paper.
- **Who signs:** the patient, or the health care agent or guardian if the patient can no longer sign. A licensed health care provider and a witness also sign.
- **Bracelet:** allowed. It goes on the wrist or ankle, has an orange background, and says "Do Not Resuscitate" in bold type.
- It covers only CPR in cardiac or respiratory arrest. EMS and hospital emergency department staff honor it. Direct care staff may follow it only if the patient's physician has ordered a hospice plan of care. Responders who doubt it are to resuscitate.

## Registries
- **Advance directive registry:** the Arizona Healthcare Directives Registry (AzHDR), run by Contexture. It is active, and residents file their own documents using the AzHDR Registration Agreement in the AG packet. Cost not confirmed.
- **POLST:** AzHDR accepts a POLST only when it is attached to a living will.
- **EMS DNR:** AzHDR accepts the Prehospital Medical Care Directive.

## Documents from other states
- A.R.S. § 36-3208: a directive from another state "is valid in this state if it was valid in the place where and at the time when it was adopted," to the extent it does not conflict with Arizona criminal law.
- Not confirmed: whether Arizona EMS honors an out-of-state POLST or DNR in place of the orange form.

## Also in this state
- **Mental Health Care Power of Attorney** (A.R.S. §§ 36-3281 to 36-3287). It is in the AG packet. It has the same witness and notary rules: one adult witness or a notary, who may not be the agent or a professional care provider directly involved in the person's care; a lone witness may not be a relative or heir. The agent may not be a person directly involved in providing the person's health care (§ 36-3281(C)). If it lets the agent admit the person to an inpatient psychiatric facility, each such paragraph must be initialed separately (§ 36-3282).
