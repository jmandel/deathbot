# Kentucky (KY)

Checked 2026-11-14.

## Naming someone to speak for you
- **Form:** the statutory "Advance Directive" in KRS 311.625(1), Kentucky Living Will Directive Act (KRS 311.621-311.643). It combines a "Living Will Directive" with a "Health Care Surrogate Designation". It also covers life-prolonging treatment, artificially provided nutrition and hydration, and organ donation. The state form is not mandatory. The Attorney General posts it as the "Living Will" packet: https://ag.ky.gov/AG%20Publications/livingwillpacket.pdf, with large-font and Spanish versions linked from https://ag.ky.gov/Resources/Consumer-Resources/seniors/Pages/Living-Wills-in-Kentucky.aspx.
- **Signing:** the document must be in writing, dated and signed. Then either two or more adults witness it, in the presence of the person and each other, or it is acknowledged before a notary (KRS 311.625(2)).
- **Who cannot witness or notarize:** a blood relative of the person; anyone who would inherit under Kentucky's descent and distribution law; the attending physician; anyone directly financially responsible for the person's health care. An employee of a health care facility where the person is a patient may not witness, but may act as the notary.
- **Who cannot be the agent:** an employee, owner, director or officer of a health care facility where the person is a resident or patient. The exceptions are a relative within the fourth degree of consanguinity or affinity, or a member of the same religious or fraternal order (KRS 311.625(4)). A surrogate may resign by written notice (311.625(3)).
- **The agent's acceptance:** not required.
- **Default decision-maker:** KRS 311.631. In order: a guardian whose authority covers medical decisions; an attorney-in-fact with health care authority; the spouse; an adult child, or a majority of the reasonably available adult children; the parents; the nearest living relative, or a majority of equally near relatives; then an adult friend who has kept regular contact and knows the person's activities, health, and religious and moral beliefs (friend class added by 2024 Ky. Acts ch. 138, HB 385).

## Written instructions (living will)
- The Living Will Directive is part of the same KRS 311.625 form, signed the same way.
- **Tube feeding:** a surrogate may withhold or withdraw artificially provided nutrition and hydration only in four situations: death is imminent (expected within a few days); the person is permanently unconscious and the directive allows it; the body cannot absorb it; or the burden outweighs the benefit. Even then, it may not be stopped if it is needed for comfort or pain relief (KRS 311.629(3)).
- **Pregnancy:** life-sustaining treatment and nutrition continue for a pregnant woman. The exception is when two physicians certify that treatment will not keep the pregnancy going to live birth, will physically harm her, or will prolong severe pain that medication cannot relieve (KRS 311.629(4)).

## Medical orders for the seriously ill (POLST-type)
- **Program:** Kentucky MOST (Medical Orders for Scope of Treatment), KRS 311.6225 and 201 KAR 9:470. The Kentucky Board of Medical Licensure publishes the form, and the Kentucky MOST Coalition promotes it (https://www.kymost.org/). It is printed on pink paper.
- **Who signs:** the patient's physician must review and sign it. The statute names no APRN or PA. The patient, surrogate or responsible party also signs. If that person is not available to sign the original, the physician may sign a copy bearing that person's signature.
- **Copies:** "Copies of the original form are equally as valid as the original form." A facsimile, paper or electronic copy is legally valid, and e-signed documents are valid (amended 2022 and July 15, 2024).

## Out-of-hospital do-not-resuscitate order
- **Form:** "Kentucky Emergency Medical Services Do Not Resuscitate (DNR) Order", KRS 311.623, standardized by the Kentucky Board of Medical Licensure (current version approved March 2024): https://kbems.ky.gov/About/Forms/DNR_Form_no_notary_no_witnesses.pdf. The person, or the legal surrogate, signs and dates it. No witnesses, notary or physician signature are required.
- **Bracelet:** a bracelet insert at the bottom of the form is cut out and placed in a hospital-type bracelet on the wrist or ankle. EMS must be shown the original form, the bracelet, or a copy with the verification lines on the form completed. Otherwise EMS may begin resuscitation.

## Registries
- Advance directive registry: unclear. A provider-only Kentucky Health Information Exchange (KHIE) registry was dropped from the 2027 readiness filing, and residents cannot file.
- POLST registry: none.
- EMS DNR registry: none. The form and bracelet are used instead.

## Documents from other states
- Not confirmed: Kentucky's living will act has no explicit rule on directives from other states. The presumption of validity in KRS 311.635 covers only directives made under KRS 311.621-311.629.

## Also in this state
