# Hawaii (HI)

Checked 2026-11-14.

## Naming someone to speak for you
- **Form:** "Advance Health-Care Directive", Haw. Rev. Stat. ch. 327E (Uniform Health-Care Decisions Act (Modified)). Part 1 of the directive is the "power of attorney for health care" (§ 327E-3(b)). The optional form in § 327E-16 may be copied, changed, or replaced by a different form with the same substance. It has four parts: Part 1 power of attorney, Part 2 instructions, Part 3 organ donation, and Part 4 primary physician. Kōkua Mau, the state's advance care planning coalition, distributes copies.
- **Signing:** the power of attorney must be dated and signed. It is then witnessed one of two ways: two adults each watch the signing or the person's acknowledgment of the signature, or the person acknowledges it before a notary public in Hawaii (§ 327E-3(b)). The form says the witnesses must know the person personally and sign under penalty of false swearing.
- **Who cannot witness:** a health-care provider; an employee of a health-care provider or facility; the agent (§ 327E-3(c)). At least one witness must be neither related to the person by blood, marriage or adoption, nor entitled to any part of the estate (§ 327E-3(d)).
- **Who cannot be the agent:** an owner, operator or employee of the health-care institution where the person is receiving care, unless related by blood, marriage or adoption (§ 327E-3(b)).
- **The agent's acceptance:** not required.
- **Default decision-maker:** Hawaii has no ranked list. A patient can name a surrogate by telling the supervising provider (§ 327E-5(a)). Otherwise the primary physician finds "interested persons": spouse (unless legally separated or estranged), reciprocal beneficiary, adult children, parents, adult siblings, adult grandchildren, and any adult who has shown special care and knows the person's values (§ 327E-2). They must try to agree among themselves on one surrogate. If they cannot agree, any of them may start guardianship proceedings (§ 327E-5(d)). A surrogate the patient did not name may stop tube feeding only if two physicians certify that it merely prolongs dying and that a future neurological response is highly unlikely (§ 327E-5(g)). The surrogate signs a written declaration of authority (§ 327E-5(j)).

## Written instructions (living will)
- Hawaii calls these "individual instructions", in Part 2 of the same § 327E-16 form. They may be written or oral (§ 327E-3(a)). The form's witness rules attach to the power of attorney, and Part 2 is signed in the same document.
- Part 2 has choices to prolong life or not, and a separate line for artificial nutrition and hydration. A box directs pain relief "even if it hastens my death."

## Medical orders for the seriously ill (POLST-type)
- **Program:** Hawaii POLST (Provider Orders for Life-Sustaining Treatment), Haw. Rev. Stat. ch. 327K. Kōkua Mau runs the program for the Department of Health. The current form was approved in May 2023, and earlier versions stay valid.
- **Who signs:** a Hawaii-licensed physician, APRN or PA, and the patient or the patient's legally authorized representative (§ 327K-1, -2). EMS and emergency physicians must follow a properly signed form (§ 327K-2(c)).
- The public can download the form at https://kokuamau.org/wp-content/uploads/Hawaii_POLST_Form_fillable.pdf. It must be printed and signed by hand. The Department of Health says electronic signatures are not accepted. A copy on white paper is legal. The original is lime green.

## Out-of-hospital do-not-resuscitate order
- The Comfort Care Only-Do Not Resuscitate (CCO-DNR) bracelet and necklace program has been suspended indefinitely. POLST is the current out-of-hospital order. Not confirmed: whether EMS still honors existing CCO-DNR bracelets.

## Registries
- Advance directive registry: none found.
- POLST registry: none.
- EMS DNR registry: none. The CCO-DNR bracelet program is suspended.

## Documents from other states
- § 327E-3(j): an advance health-care directive is valid if it complies with ch. 327E, or if it complied with the law of the state where it was signed. Not confirmed: any Hawaii rule on out-of-state POLST forms. Kōkua Mau only points to polst.org.

## Also in this state
- Advance Mental Health Care Directive, Haw. Rev. Stat. ch. 327G. It gives mental health treatment instructions and can name a mental health agent. It may be combined with the ch. 327E directive (§ 327G-3).
