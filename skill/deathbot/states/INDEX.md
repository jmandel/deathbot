## Low confidence

No state was rated low confidence. These were rated medium; each file's "Not confirmed:" lines give the open points:

- AL: official text of §§ 22-8A-4, -11 and -12 not reached; enrolled act number for the 2022 witness change.
- AK: AS 13.52.010, .030 and .150 not opened; signing rules come from the statutory form.
- AR: FindLaw copies only; whether APRNs or PAs may sign the POLST.
- CO: statute is silent on MDPOA witnesses; CDPHE bracelet rule not read.
- CT: statutes read from FindLaw; no out-of-state recognition rule found.
- DE: chapter 25A text as amended by SB 274 not read; form not yet reissued under the POLST name.
- GA: whether 2025 S.B. 153 changed § 31-9-2; § 31-32-5 checked only through the state form.
- IL: current wording of 755 ILCS 40/25 and 35/3 not reached.
- IN: POST electronic signatures and out-of-state POST rule.
- KS: whether an APRN may sign TPOPP.
- KY: KRS 311.625 read only through FindLaw; out-of-state recognition.
- LA: no witness rule found for a health care mandate; whether NPs or PAs may sign LaPOST.
- ME: who signs the Maine POLST rests on secondary sources.
- MA: mass.gov blocked; MOLST and Comfort Care/DNR details rest on search excerpts.
