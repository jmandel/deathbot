Talk through how you want the end of your life to go.

I'm an AI. I can explain what treatments like CPR and breathing machines are really like, help you work out what you'd want, and write it down for whoever would speak for you.

To talk, this page needs to reach an AI service called OpenRouter. You sign in with your own account and pay them for what you use, usually a few cents a conversation.
